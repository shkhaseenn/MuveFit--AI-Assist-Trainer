# MuveFit

## Camera-Based Movement Analysis & Body Awareness

MuveFit is a camera-based movement analysis platform designed to help users understand how they move, evaluate exercise form, and build greater awareness of their body.

The platform combines a modern fitness interface with pose-based movement analysis, exercise feedback, workout history, and privacy-conscious design.

---
## Product Vision

> See how you move. Understand your form. Become more aware of your body.

MuveFit turns camera-based movement data into simple and understandable exercise insights.

The core experience is:

```text
Choose Exercise
      ↓
Open Camera
      ↓
Move
      ↓
Pose Detection
      ↓
Movement Analysis
      ↓
Form + Accuracy Feedback
      ↓
Workout Result
      ↓
History & Progress
